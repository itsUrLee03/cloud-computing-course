# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

### 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?
*  **   **
Google Cloud Platform (GCP) impressed me the most due to its clean, developer-friendly ecosystem and native focus on cutting-edge technologies. While AWS dominates in sheer market size and Azure leads in corporate software integration, GCP stands out with its seamless management of containerized workloads through Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) and its accessible AI development suite via Vertex AI.



### 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?
  *   **    **
Despite their unique branding, all three providers build on the exact same core cloud computing primitives: scalable virtual compute instances, object and block storage solutions, virtual private networking, and granular Identity and Access Management (IAM) controls. Additionally, all three organize their global infrastructure around geographic regions and isolated availability zones to guarantee redundancy, fault tolerance, and high availability.



### 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?
 *   **   ** 
Business requirements dictate cloud platform selection by aligning an organization's specific operational constraints—such as existing IT investments, staff skill sets, compliance needs, and budget—with the provider's strengths. For instance, an enterprise heavily invested in Microsoft software and Active Directory achieves greater cost-efficiency using Azure, whereas an AI startup prioritizes GCP's specialized TPU compute clusters to accelerate model training.



### 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?
 *   **  ** 
Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to evaluate technology through a pragmatic business lens rather than looking purely at technical specifications. I learned to analyze client requirements, identify operational pain points, and map specific business constraints directly to tailored, cost-effective cloud architectural decisions.
